How VoltWise works

An EV's range estimate is only as good as its battery-management system (BMS). State of charge (SOC) cannot be measured directly — it has to be estimated from noisy current and voltage sensors, with a model of the cell.

1 · Cell model

Each of the 96 series cell groups (2 × 50 Ah NMC cells in parallel) is a first-order Thevenin equivalent circuit:

SOC' = −I / (3600·Q) v₁' = −v₁/τ + I/C₁ (τ = R₁C₁ ≈ 20 s) V = OCV(SOC) − v₁ − R₀(SOC, T)·I

OCV(SOC) is a monotone cubic (PCHIP) fit to a typical NMC curve; R₀ rises at low SOC and in the cold (Arrhenius). A lumped thermal model heats each cell with I²R₀ + v₁²/R₁ and cools it to ambient. Cells differ by a manufacturing spread in capacity and resistance.

2 · Vehicle

P = (m·a + ½ρC_dA·v² + C_rr·m·g)·v / η (+ auxiliaries), regen: P·η_regen

Procedural city, highway and sporty drive cycles produce the battery current.

3 · Estimators

  • Coulomb counting integrates measured current — exact in theory, but it never corrects a wrong starting value and integrates every sensor offset and capacity error forever.
  • Voltage lookup inverts the OCV curve — self-correcting, but noisy under load and blind in flat parts of the curve.
  • Extended Kalman filter fuses both: predicts with coulomb counting and the RC model, corrects with the voltage measurement through the linearised OCV slope, and tracks its own uncertainty (±3σ band).
  • EKF + bias state additionally estimates the current-sensor offset as a third state.

4 · Charging & balancing

Charging is constant-current until the highest cell reaches 4.2 V, then constant-voltage. In a series string the pack is empty when the weakest cell is empty and full when the strongest is full — energy left in the other cells is stranded. Passive balancing bleeds high cells through resistors so the string ends up aligned.

Things to try

  • Start the BMS at 60 % while the pack is at 85 %: coulomb counting stays 25 % off; the EKF converges within minutes.
  • Raise the current-sensor offset and drive for an hour: the plain EKF drifts slightly, the bias-state EKF learns the offset.
  • Drop the ambient temperature to −10 °C and drive the sporty cycle.
  • Switch to Charge with balancing off, then on, and watch the cell spread at the top.
